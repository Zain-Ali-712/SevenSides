## Brand
- **Name / logo:** "SEVEN SIDES", stacked uppercase wordmark set in **Bebas Neue** (verified against profile logo; free on Google Fonts).
- **Tagline:** "Home of Red Tenders" (IG bio, Lake City launch poster).
- **Positioning (their words):** "Seven Sides being the first brand to introduce the Tenders & Sando" · "Before everyone started calling sandwiches Sando and before Nashville tenders became a staple item in every fast food menu, there was SEVEN SIDES" (FB, 2026-05-11). Hashtags: #nashville #nashvillefriedchicken.
- **Heat scale (official):** No Heat → Country Heat → Screamer.
- **Voice:** playful, Gen-Z, Lahore slang ("Your next situationship", "Third branch unlocked 🔓", "Are You Ready to Handle the Heat", "Caution ⚠️ Extremely Flavourful").
